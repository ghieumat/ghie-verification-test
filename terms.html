STUDENT INFORMATION VERIFICATION PORTAL

Terms of Service

Effective date: 29 October 2026

These terms explain the permitted use of the GhIE–UMaT Student Chapter's Student Information Verification Portal. The portal supports the 2026 registration-information verification process.

Important

This portal verifies registration information only. It does not accept payments, confirm payment clearance, issue a Member ID, or itself complete official GhIE headquarters registration.

1. Who may use the portal

Students may enter their registered email and original reference number to open a short-lived, record-scoped verification session. These are identifiers, not secure identity credentials; this simplified method allows viewing details, photographs and requesting changes without independently proving ownership. The chapter acknowledges the resulting privacy and impersonation risk. Administrator functions require separate server-authorized password login.

Use only your own registration identifiers. Do not attempt to locate or access another student's record. Administrators must not share passwords or sessions.

2. Accurate information

Review the displayed information carefully. When you confirm your details, you are stating that you have reviewed them and believe them to be accurate. A numeric-only reference number passes a format check but is not, by itself, proof that the number is correct.

If a field is wrong, submit a correction proposal with the value you believe is correct. The original submission is preserved. A proposed correction remains pending until an authorized administrator reviews it; submission does not guarantee approval or change the official record immediately.

3. Safe and permitted use

You agree not to:

  • impersonate another student or administrator;
  • try to access records, photographs, or administrative functions you are not authorized to access;
  • bypass authentication, alter requests to evade access controls, scrape student records, or automate excessive requests;
  • interfere with the portal, its hosting, or its security controls; or
  • submit malicious, unlawful, or deliberately misleading information.

4. Registration identifiers and administrator security

The student access form does not independently establish the identity of the person submitting it. It provides a limited-scope session for the matched registration; you must only use identifiers belonging to your own registration. Anyone who knows another student’s identifiers could potentially impersonate that student, so users and chapter administrators must treat this as a reduced-security access method.

Administrators must use individually assigned credentials and sign out on shared devices. Authentication can expire and require a new login after a refresh. Never send your administrator password or session token to anyone.

5. Submissions, timeouts, and duplicate actions

Wait for the portal to confirm a submission. If a request times out, the result may be uncertain: the server may have completed the operation even if the browser did not receive the response. Follow the on-screen status-check or safe-retry instructions. Do not repeatedly submit a different request to try to force a result.

The portal uses request identifiers to reduce duplicate operations, but network or service interruptions can still occur. A timeout message is not proof that a request failed, and a loading message is not proof that it succeeded.

6. No payment or financial-clearance service

The portal does not collect or process registration fees, approve transactions, change payment approval status, or send payment-related SMS notifications. Do not submit payment card details, mobile-money credentials, transaction receipts, or payer-account details through this portal. Use only the separately communicated official payment process where applicable.

7. Service availability and external services

The portal depends on Google Apps Script, Google Sheets/Drive, network connectivity, and GitHub Pages hosting. It may be delayed, interrupted, or unavailable during maintenance or third-party service incidents. The chapter may suspend access where needed to protect records or investigate misuse.

8. Registration outcome

The portal helps prepare and verify information. A successful confirmation or an approved correction is not, by itself, proof of membership, payment clearance, assignment of an official Member ID, issuance of an E-Card, or acceptance by GhIE headquarters. Those outcomes depend on the relevant official process.

9. Privacy

Your use of the portal is also covered by the Privacy Policy, which explains the information handled and the reasons for processing it.

10. Changes and applicable law

The chapter may update these terms when the portal or registration process changes. Updated terms will be posted here with a revised effective date. These terms are intended to be read consistently with applicable laws of Ghana and do not remove rights that cannot lawfully be limited.

11. Questions

For questions about use of the portal, contact the GhIE–UMaT Student Chapter through the official chapter communication channel through which you received your verification invitation.
